Moonhollow Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how the Moonhollow game ("the app") handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Moonhollow has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server operated by us. We cannot see how you play or who you are.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (your village, items and settings) and a record of the in-app purchases you have received are saved only on your device, so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device through the app. Deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details, name or email address. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Moonhollow is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects no personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
