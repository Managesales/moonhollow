Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Store (bottom-right of the screen) and tap Restore purchases at the top right. The Starter Bundle, Moon Familiar and Cellar Charm are restored to the same Apple ID. Moonstone packs are used up when spent, so they can't be restored.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. The game saves automatically every few seconds and whenever you leave the app. Progress is stored on your device, so deleting the app also deletes your village.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Moonhollow plays fully offline. You only need a connection to make or restore purchases.

Do timers keep running when the game is closed?

Yes. Crops, workshops and home tribute keep progressing while you are away.

I was charged but didn't receive my item.

Close and reopen the game; any purchase that didn't finish is delivered on the next launch. If it still hasn't arrived, email us. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
